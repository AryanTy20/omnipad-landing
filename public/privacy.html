Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers the Omnipad Android app (package app.omnipad) and the Omnipad desktop app. Omnipad has no accounts and the developer runs no servers that receive your data.

What you type and control

Keystrokes, mouse movement, macros, media commands and scanned codes are sent only to the device you connect to, over Bluetooth, your local Wi-Fi network or USB. They are not sent to the developer or any third party, and they are not stored.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings, tile boards, recent devices, pairing tokens and Pro status are stored locally on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth (Nearby devices): to act as a Bluetooth keyboard, mouse, remote and gamepad, and to pair with your computer or TV.
  • Network and Wi-Fi: to find and connect to the Omnipad desktop app on your network, and to load ads.
  • Camera: only for Scan to type. Images are processed on the phone and never uploaded.
  • Microphone (voice typing): handled by your phone's system speech recognizer (for example Google). Its provider's privacy policy applies.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep the connection alive while Omnipad is minimised.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Omnipad under Settings → Unlock Pro → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Pro Lifetime is bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; the developer receives no card details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own Pro.

Children

Omnipad is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/AryanTy20/omnipad/issues.
